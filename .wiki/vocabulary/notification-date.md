# Overview

The date the member was notified of the [Transaction](transaction.md), when
the [Filing](filing.md) states it. Not every Filing states it structurally:
it is a printed field on the Filing line, but may instead surface only
inside free-text remarks, or not at all.[^context-doc] Avoid: Disclosure
date.
